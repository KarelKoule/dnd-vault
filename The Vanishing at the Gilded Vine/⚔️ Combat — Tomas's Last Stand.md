# ⚔️ Combat — Tomas's Last Stand

*Triggered when Tomas is cornered and panics. He'd rather escape than die.*

---

## Encounter

**Location:** Wine cellar beneath the warehouse — barrels, low ceiling, one exit (the stairs).

**Tomas** — use **Spy** stat block (CR 1)
- Prioritizes the exit over killing anyone
- If reduced below half HP: attempts to bribe, then flee
- If escape is blocked: surrenders

**2× Cellar Thugs** — use **Bandit** stat block (CR ⅛)
- Hired muscle, not loyal — if Tomas goes down or flees, they drop weapons

> **Spicier version:** swap Bandits for **Thug** (CR ½) ×2. Moderate fight, still safe for three level-3 characters.

---

## Aldric

Tied up in the corner. Not in danger during the fight — Tomas needs him alive as leverage. If Tomas is losing, he may threaten Aldric to buy an escape.

---

## After the Fight

Aldric is freed. He's grumpy, embarrassed, and has a lot of explaining to do — both to his wife and about his gambling debts. Whether the party lets the loan shark collect is their call.
