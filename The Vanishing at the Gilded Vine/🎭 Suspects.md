# 🎭 Suspects

*Hand each guest a card with just these four lines. Tell them their secret privately. They only ever talk — never roll, never fight.*

---

## Tomas
*The business partner — THE CULPRIT*

**Personality:** Smooth, generous, keeps refilling everyone's glass.
**Secret:** You did it. Aldric is tied up in the wine cellar beneath the warehouse.
**Your lie:** "I was at the festival all night — half the town saw me."
**Crack under pressure if:** confronted with the muddy boots AND Sera's testimony.
Then: bribe → flee → *(optional)* fight → [[⚔️ Combat — Tomas's Last Stand]]

---

## Mirela
*The wife*

**Personality:** Dramatic mourning, but her eyes don't match her words.
**Secret:** You were planning to leave Aldric. You're secretly relieved — and you feel guilty about that.
**Your lie:** "My husband had no enemies. Everyone adored him."
**Truth she'll give if pressed kindly:** Aldric was acting nervous and secretive all week.

---

## Bram
*"Lucky" Bram — the bookkeeper*

**Personality:** Sweaty, fidgety, laughs too loud.
**Secret:** Aldric owed big gambling debts — and you've been quietly skimming money to cover your own.
**Your lie:** "The books are perfectly in order. Perfectly."
**Truth he'll give if cornered:** Points the party toward the local loan shark *(red herring!)*.

---

## Cass
*The festival wine-taster / sommelier — OPTIONAL 4th card*

**Personality:** Flamboyant, name-drops constantly, treats the investigation as beneath them — but is secretly thrilled to be involved in scandal.
**Secret:** You were sneaking around the warehouse that night to steal a bottle of legendary reserve vintage to win a bet. You saw more than you admit.
**Your lie:** "I was nowhere near the warehouse. I was judging the cheese pairings, darling."
**Truth if cornered or flattered:** "Fine — I was there. And I saw Tomas. Carrying something heavy toward the cellar. I assumed it was a wine cask…"
→ Directly corroborates Sera and cracks Tomas's alibi.

**How Cass fits the clue chain:**

| Clue | How to find it | Leads to |
|------|---------------|----------|
| Cass was seen near the warehouse | Festival NPC or Investigation DC 13 | Second suspicious lurker — muddies suspicion |
| Cass's eyewitness account | Persuasion/Performance DC 12 or stroke their ego | Confirms Tomas hauled a heavy "cask" to the cellar |

> **If Cass isn't needed:** fold their eyewitness line into Sera — no rewrite required.

---

## Sera
*The maid — DM-run NPC*

**Personality:** Timid, terrified.
**What she knows:** Saw a stranger near the cellar and found the muddy boots.
**Gate:** She only talks if the party is **gentle** — no Intimidation. Kindness/Persuasion DC 13 only.

> Keep Sera in your hands so you control exactly when this key clue drops.

---

## Suspect Map

| Suspect | Role | Points toward |
|---------|------|--------------|
| Tomas | Culprit | Lies about alibi |
| Mirela | Emotional misdirection | Relieved widow |
| Bram | Red herring | Debt → loan shark |
| Cass | False second suspect | Ultimately breaks the case — fingers Tomas |

Natural escalation: party suspects Bram (debts) → then Cass (lurking) → pressing Cass kindly pivots to Tomas.
