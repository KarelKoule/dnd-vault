# 🍷 The Vanishing at the Gilded Vine

*A side-quest one-shot for 3 PCs (level 3–4) + up to 3–4 guest players*

---

## Setup

The party arrives at a town mid-Harvest Festival — banners, music, free tastings. Then a bell rings: **Aldric Vass**, the town's celebrated wine merchant, has vanished from his locked study during the festival. His wife offers **150 gold** for his safe return. The town watch is useless and overwhelmed by festival crowds.

**The scene:** a study locked from the inside. Inside: an overturned chair, a half-finished letter, and one muddy boot-print too large to be Aldric's.

---

## The Truth (DM only)

Aldric **faked his own disappearance** to dodge gambling debts — but before he could slip out of town, his business partner **Tomas abducted him for real** to seize the vineyard. Aldric is alive, tied up and grumpy in the wine cellar beneath his own warehouse.

- The locked room: Aldric left through a **hidden servant's passage** (his own fake-out)
- Tomas grabbed him in the **alley outside**
- The muddy boot-print belongs to **Tomas**

---

## Clue Chain

| Clue | How to find it | Leads to |
|------|---------------|----------|
| Half-finished letter | Just read it (no roll) | Mentions "debt come due" → points at Bram / loan shark |
| Muddy boot-print | Investigation DC 13 | Too large for Aldric — someone else was here |
| Hidden servant's passage | Perception DC 14 (auto if they search walls) | Aldric left on his own — twist! |
| Bram's cooked books | Investigation DC 14 or social pressure | Debts real, but Bram's the skimmer (red herring) |
| Sera's testimony | Persuasion DC 13 + kindness only | Saw a man drag a sack toward the warehouse cellar |
| Boots match Tomas | Insight DC 13 on Tomas's lie | Points to the real culprit |
| Wine cellar | Follow the trail | 🎯 Aldric, alive and tied up |

> **Keep it forgiving:** with only 3 investigators, let reasonable roleplay shortcut a roll. If stuck, Sera or a festival drunk can nudge them.

## Finale — Pick Your Ending

When the party corners Tomas with the boots + Sera's testimony:

**① Pure social** *(default)*
Tomas folds, confesses, begs, offers a bribe. Aldric is freed.

**② Light combat**
Tomas panics and fights. → [[⚔️ Combat — Tomas's Last Stand]]

**③ Three-way twist**
As Tomas is cornered, the loan shark arrives to collect Aldric's real debt. Negotiation standoff — who gets Aldric, and at what price?

---

## Table Notes

- 3 investigators : 3 suspects = clean 1:1 dynamic. Regulars interrogate; guests perform.
- Guests never touch a rulebook — just a secret and a lie.
- Combat is optional and trimmed safe for three level-3 characters.
- Drops into any campaign as "we stopped in a town for the festival."
- **Runtime:** ~90 minutes to 2 hours.
