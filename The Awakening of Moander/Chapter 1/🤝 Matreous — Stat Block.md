### How to play Matreous in combat:

This is the key — make him **supportively reliable but not a one-man-army.**

**Round 1:** Casts _**Bless**_ on the three PCs. (+1d4 to attack rolls and saves — _huge_ for the party's hit chances.) Concentration.

**Round 2:** _**Healing Word**_ (bonus action) on the most-injured PC; _**Sacred Flame**_ at Vessen (action).

**Round 3+:**

- If a PC is below half HP: _**Cure Wounds**_ (in melee) or _**Healing Word**_ (at range).
- If Bless drops: re-cast it.
- Otherwise: _**Sacred Flame**_ at the most dangerous enemy.

**If targeted directly:** He moves behind a PC for cover, using the wagon if available. If he drops to 0 HP, he is **stable but unconscious** — do not kill him. The cultists, if they see him drop, will **break off** from him (their job is "make sure he doesn't reach Hollowford" — incapacitated counts).

**Resource usage:** He has plenty of slots for this fight. Plan to have him use ~2× 1st-level slots and ~1× 2nd-level slot total. He'll arrive at Hollowford fresh enough to be useful there too.
