
Two days into the four-day journey from Candlekeep to Hollowford. 

---

> _The road narrows here, where old beeches lean inward and the afternoon light falls in green-gold patches. Matreous is humming an old hymn under his breath, tapping the rhythm on the side of the wagon. A jay calls somewhere ahead. Somewhere else, much closer, a jay calls back — and falls suddenly silent._
> 
> _Matreous stops humming. "Friends," he says quietly, "I do not think we are alone."_

### Combat Encounter (scaled for 4 level-1 PCs)

[[🗡️ Ambushers]]

[[🤝 Matreous — Stat Block]]

After the fight, he is shaken but composed. He tends wounds. He thanks the party with quiet sincerity. **He does not yet realize what the attack means.**

## 🔍 What the Party Finds on the Bodies


### On the bandit-cultists (obvious clues):

- Rough bandit gear _over_ **dark robes** with **a small embroidered symbol**: a stylized dragon's eye. _(The Cult of the Dragon's mark — players who've encountered the cult before recognize it. Players new to FR are told by Matreous who recognizes it.)_

### On the acolyte (the real clues):

- The **silver scale-medallion** — clearly a cult rank-token.
- A folded, sealed letter — **purple wax seal in the shape of a dragon's eye** _

> _"Bratře Vessene,_
>
> Ucenec Matreous jede z Candlekeep po Way of Lion, právě když píšu. Nesmí dorazit. At to vypadat tak, jak to někdy vypadá na cestě – bandité, smůla, neštěstí starého muže. Neberte žádné zajatce. Nenechávejte po sobě nic.
>
> _Slovo je téměř proneseno. Nesmíme být do té doby slyšeni._
>
> _— Y."_

### What Matreous says when he sees the evidence:

> _"Cult of the Dragon… here? On a road where they have no business?"_
> 
> _He is quiet for a long moment, turning the medallion in his fingers._
> 
> *"Friends. I came to lift a curse. A curse is a small thing — a thing of one wronged party, one bitter word, one bound spirit. Curses do not send assassins. Curses do not write letters. **Whatever is happening in Hollowford, it is not a curse.** Or — it is, but it is a curse with **hands.**"*
> 
> _He looks up, and there is something in his face the party has not seen before — not fear, but a kind of **focused gravity.**_
> 
> _"I think I am still the man you need. But I think you are also the people I need. Shall we continue?"_

This is your **act break** — the moment the campaign's tone shifts. The party knows something larger is happening _before_ they reach Hollowford, which means they arrive in the village **already in investigation mode**, already protective of Matreous, already alert to strangers. Yarrow's friendly smile at the inn now reads very differently.
