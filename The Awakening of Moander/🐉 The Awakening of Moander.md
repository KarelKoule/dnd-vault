
Sammaster, in his lich form

- His goal is unchanged: **devour Moander's residual divinity and ascend.**

The path requires [[📜 The Seven Whispers]], each a distinct stage of magical work, performed in a specific sequence. But unlike a single ritual at a single site, **most Whispers are themselves networks of rituals** — multiple parallel ceremonies at multiple sites, accumulating energy that Sammaster channels toward the next stage.

This means:

- **Early Whispers** = many small rituals, low-level cultists, scattered across Faerûn → **perfect for level 1–6 play.**
- **Middle Whispers** = fewer but larger rituals, mid-tier cult lieutenants and dragons → **level 7–12 play.**
- **Final Whispers** = singular, massive rituals, performed by Sammaster himself → **level 13+ climax.**
